# Pharma analyst report template

Use this outline selectively. Omit empty sections and lead with what matters to the user's decision.

## Header

- Subject:
- Report type:
- Report date:
- Data cutoff (`As of`):
- Geography/currency:
- Scope and key assumptions:

## Executive view

State the conclusion in 3-6 bullets:

- Core thesis
- Strongest evidence
- Largest uncertainty
- Most important catalyst
- Valuation or strategic implication, if in scope

Include a compact dashboard when useful:

| Metric | Current view | Evidence grade | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|---|

## 1. Company and strategy

- Identity, ownership, leadership, financing, runway
- Business model and strategic positioning
- Partnerships and rights
- Timeline of value-inflecting events

## 2. Platform

- Architecture and claimed advantage
- Evidence ladder achieved
- Platform-to-asset translation
- Reproducibility, scalability, and limitations

## 3. Pipeline

| Asset | Target/MoA | Modality | Indication | Verified stage | Trial ID | Next catalyst |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Flag discrepancies between registries and company materials.

## 4. Lead-asset deep dive

### Biology and mechanism

- Disease rationale
- Human validation
- Mechanistic advantages and liabilities

### Molecule and translational package

- Design and developability
- Preclinical evidence with model limitations
- PK/PD and biomarker strategy

### Clinical development

| Item | Detail | Analyst interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| Design/population | | |
| Dose/exposure | | |
| Endpoints | | |
| Efficacy | | |
| Safety | | |
| Immunogenicity | | |
| Limitations | | |

### Differentiation

Compare with relevant standard of care and pipeline competitors. Label cross-trial comparisons.

### Asset verdict

- What is established
- What remains a company claim
- What the next experiment or readout must show
- Evidence grade and confidence

Repeat for other material assets at proportionate depth.

## 5. Competitive landscape

| Therapy | Company | Mechanism | Relevant indication/stage | Key evidence | Differentiation | Limitation |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Discuss how the likely standard of care may change before launch.

## 6. Market and commercialization

- Patient funnel and addressable segments
- Pricing/access analogues
- Adoption drivers and barriers
- Commercial model and partnering needs

## 7. Regulatory, CMC, and IP

Present regulator-confirmed facts first. Separate CMC risks from biological risks. Label patent/FTO discussion as non-legal research intelligence.

## 8. Valuation or strategic scenarios

| Assumption | Bear | Base | Bull | Source/rationale |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|

Show indication-level value, probability adjustments, net cash/debt, dilution or financing assumptions, and sensitivity analysis. For a private company, emphasize ranges and financing scenarios.

## 9. Risks and falsifiers

| Risk | Severity | Evidence | Signpost | Thesis impact |
|---|---|---|---|---|

State at least one falsifier for each central thesis claim.

## 10. Catalyst calendar

| Window | Catalyst | What to watch | Upside/downside interpretation |
|---|---|---|---|

## 11. Conclusion and monitoring plan

- Current conclusion
- Unresolved questions
- Highest-value next diligence steps
- Scheduled monitoring items

## Sources and limitations

List primary sources first. For each major dataset include publication date and data cutoff. State missing information, source conflicts, and areas based on inference or scenario assumptions.

Add: `This report is for research purposes and is not medical, legal, patent, or personalized investment advice.`
